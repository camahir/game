How to play

Bloc, in four rules.

01

Reach the far row. You start at the bottom, your opponent at the top. First pawn to cross to the opposite side wins.

02

One action per turn. Either step your pawn to an adjacent open cell, or drop a wall panel across a gap between cells.

03

Ten walls each. A wall blocks movement across that edge for both players. Once placed, it stays.

04

Never a dead end. You can't place a wall that would leave either player with zero path to their goal — the board will refuse it.

05

Leaping. If your opponent is directly next to you, you may jump straight over them. If something blocks that jump, you can hop diagonally instead.
